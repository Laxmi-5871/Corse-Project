Help Center

🔍 How do I report a lost item? ›
Go to the Lost section from the home page, tap "Report Lost Item" and fill in the item title, category, location, date, and upload a photo if available.
👜 How do I report a found item? ›
Go to Found section and tap "Report Found Item". Fill in all details. The original owner will be notified if there's a match.
🤝 How does matching work? ›
Our system compares lost and found item descriptions, categories, locations, and dates to suggest possible matches. Both parties get notified. Check the Matches tab regularly.
🛒 How do I buy or sell items? ›
Go to Buy/Sell from the home page. List items for sale with photos and a price, or browse what others are selling. Contact the seller to arrange payment and pickup.
💳 How do payments work? ›
Payments are peer-to-peer between buyers and sellers. You can pay via UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), bank transfer, or cash on campus. Set up your method in Payment & Wallet settings.
👤 Who can use this app? ›
Only KLE Technological University students with a valid @kletech.ac.in email can register and use the platform.
📞 How to contact support? ›
Email us at support@kletech-lostandfound.in or visit the IT Helpdesk at Admin Block, Room 102. We respond within 24 hours.

About This App

🏫
KLETech Lost & Found
Version 1.0.0 · For students, by students

What is this app?

KLETech Lost & Found is a campus platform for KLE Technological University students to report lost and found items, buy/sell goods, and get AI-powered matches — all in one place.

Our Mission

To make campus life easier by helping students recover lost belongings and reduce waste through a smart campus reuse marketplace.

How Payments Work

All payments are peer-to-peer — directly between buyers and sellers. We support UPI, bank transfers, and cash handovers. We don't hold any funds or charge any commission.

Developed By
